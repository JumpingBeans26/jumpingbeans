# Jumping Beans Coffee — Brand & Style Reference

Use this alongside `design-tokens.json` when building the companion app, so it matches the website.

## Logo
`jumping-beans-logo.png` — circular badge mark. Use as app icon, splash screen, and nav bar mark.

## Color Palette
Bohemian / watercolor-inspired, sampled from the logo art — warm cream, dark espresso, caramel gold, muted sage, and rust. No blue or forest green.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso | `#3B2410` | Outline/border accent, secondary dark |
| Espresso Dark | `#2A1809` | Hero/dark sections, headings, combo banner |
| Cream Deep | `#F4E8D6` | Card/section backgrounds |
| Cream | `#FBF4E8` | Page background |
| Caramel | `#C27C2E` | Primary buttons, specials banner, highlights |
| Caramel Light | `#E3A85C` | Accents on dark backgrounds, iced coffee banner |
| Coffee | `#6B4226` | Coffee banner, food banner, coffee-toned text |
| Sage | `#6E6B42` | Matcha banner (replaces forest green) |
| Terracotta | `#B2600C` | Tea banner, section labels (replaces sky blue) |
| Ink | `#2E1B0C` | Body text |

## Typography
- **Headings:** Fraunces (serif)
- **Script accents:** Great Vibes (cursive) — sparingly, for taglines only
- **Body/UI text:** Manrope (sans-serif)

## Visual Style
- Flat, solid-color banners — no glossy gradients or glow effects
- Monoline hand-drawn-style icons (thin stroke, rounded ends), not emoji or photo-realistic icons
- Subtle paper-grain texture on light backgrounds is okay; keep it understated
- Rounded corners: 10px cards, 999px pill buttons, 14px image frames

## Menu Hierarchy (for the app's ordering flow)
1. Coffee (primary/default tab)
2. Jumping Beans Specials
3. Iced Coffee
4. Tea
5. Matcha (secondary — present but not the lead category)
6. Food
7. Combo Deals
8. Perk Me Up (loyalty)

**Signature drink:** Jumping Beans Signature Salted Caramel Oat Flat White — feature this prominently (e.g. home screen banner), same treatment as on the website.

## Brand Voice / Positioning
100% plant-based. All drinks made with premium oat milk. Vegan-safe — no honey or animal-derived ingredients in any recipe or copy.
